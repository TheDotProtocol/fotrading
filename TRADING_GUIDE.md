# Trading Guide - How Different Instruments Work

## 📊 Understanding Different Trading Instruments

### 1. **Stocks** (What you already have)
**How it works:**
- Buy shares of a company (e.g., MAYBANK)
- Own a portion of the company
- Price moves based on company performance
- Can hold long-term or trade short-term

**Example:**
- Buy 100 shares of MAYBANK at MYR 9.12
- Total cost: MYR 912 + fees
- If price goes to MYR 9.50, you profit MYR 38

---

### 2. **Futures** (Contracts for Future Delivery)
**How it works:**
- Contract to buy/sell a commodity at a future date at a predetermined price
- You don't own the actual commodity (e.g., gold)
- Trade on price movements
- Use leverage (can control more with less capital)
- Must close position before expiration

**Example:**
- Buy 1 Gold Futures contract at MYR 285.50/gram
- Contract size: 100 grams
- Total value: MYR 28,550 (but you only need margin, e.g., 10% = MYR 2,855)
- If gold goes to MYR 290/gram, you profit on the difference
- Can go LONG (buy) or SHORT (sell) - profit from price going up OR down

**Key Terms:**
- **Long Position**: Betting price will go UP
- **Short Position**: Betting price will go DOWN
- **Margin**: Small deposit to control larger position
- **Expiration**: Contract expires on a specific date

---

### 3. **Forex (Foreign Exchange)** (Currency Trading)
**How it works:**
- Trade currency pairs (e.g., USD/MYR)
- Buy one currency, sell another
- Profit from exchange rate movements
- Trade in lots (standardized amounts)
- 24/5 market (open almost all the time)

**Example:**
- Buy USD/MYR at 4.72 (you're buying USD, selling MYR)
- If rate goes to 4.75, you profit
- Trade in lots: 1 lot = 100,000 units
- Mini lot = 10,000 units, Micro lot = 1,000 units

**Key Terms:**
- **Base Currency**: First currency (USD in USD/MYR)
- **Quote Currency**: Second currency (MYR in USD/MYR)
- **Pip**: Smallest price movement (0.0001 for most pairs)
- **Spread**: Difference between buy and sell price

---

### 4. **ETFs (Exchange Traded Funds)** (Like Stocks, but Diversified)
**How it works:**
- Similar to stocks - buy/sell shares
- But each share represents a basket of assets
- Trade like stocks on exchange
- Lower risk than individual stocks (diversified)

**Example:**
- Buy 100 shares of Gold ETF at MYR 1.85
- Each share represents gold holdings
- If gold price goes up, ETF price goes up
- Can trade anytime during market hours

**Key Terms:**
- **NAV**: Net Asset Value (underlying value)
- **Tracking**: ETF follows an index or commodity
- **Dividend**: Some ETFs pay dividends

---

### 5. **Bonds** (Lending Money to Government/Companies)
**How it works:**
- You're lending money (buying bond)
- Government/company pays you interest (coupon)
- Get principal back at maturity
- Can sell before maturity (price fluctuates)

**Example:**
- Buy MY10Y (10-year bond) at MYR 100
- Coupon: 3.48% per year
- You receive MYR 3.48 interest per year
- After 10 years, get MYR 100 back
- Can sell anytime - price changes with interest rates

**Key Terms:**
- **Coupon**: Annual interest payment (%)
- **Yield**: Return you get (current yield)
- **Maturity**: When you get your money back
- **Face Value**: Original price (usually MYR 100)

---

## 🎯 Trading Flow (Same for All Instruments)

1. **Choose Instrument** → Click on futures, forex, ETF, or bond
2. **Select Direction** → BUY (Long) or SELL (Short) - depends on instrument
3. **Enter Quantity** → How many contracts/lots/shares
4. **Choose Order Type** → Market (immediate) or Limit (at specific price)
5. **Review Fees** → See all costs upfront
6. **Confirm Trade** → Execute the order
7. **Monitor Position** → See P/L in portfolio

## 💡 Key Differences

| Instrument | What You Own | Leverage | Expiration | Trading Hours |
|------------|--------------|----------|------------|---------------|
| **Stocks** | Shares of company | No | None | Market hours |
| **Futures** | Contract (not asset) | Yes (high) | Yes | Extended hours |
| **Forex** | Currency position | Yes (high) | None | 24/5 |
| **ETFs** | Shares of fund | No | None | Market hours |
| **Bonds** | Debt instrument | No | Maturity date | Market hours |

---

## ⚠️ Risk Levels

1. **Low Risk**: Bonds, ETFs
2. **Medium Risk**: Stocks
3. **High Risk**: Futures, Forex (due to leverage)

**Remember**: Higher leverage = Higher risk = Higher potential profit/loss
